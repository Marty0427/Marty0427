# PocketPass Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

PocketPass does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. This policy exists to say
exactly that, and to describe what the app does with the information you put into it.

## What PocketPass stores

The cards you add — their names, issuers, barcode payloads, notes, colours and expiry dates —
are saved in a file inside the app's own storage on your device. That file is protected by iOS
file encryption and is unreadable while your device is locked.

## What PocketPass does not do

- It has no account, no sign-in and no server.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
- It makes no network requests. Nothing you store is uploaded anywhere.

## Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner screen is open, to read a barcode. Frames are
analysed on the device and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

## Photos

When you import a code from an image, PocketPass uses Apple's system photo picker. The picker
runs outside the app and hands PocketPass only the single image you select. The app has no
access to the rest of your photo library.

## Backups

An exported backup is an ordinary JSON file containing your cards, including the barcode
numbers themselves. Where you save or send it — AirDrop, Files, a cloud drive — is your choice
and falls outside the app's control. Anyone who opens that file can read the codes in it, so
treat it as you would the cards themselves.

## Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode

If you turn on the app lock, authentication is performed by iOS. PocketPass is told only
whether it succeeded; it never receives your biometric data or your passcode.

## Children

PocketPass collects no data at all, from anyone, including children.
